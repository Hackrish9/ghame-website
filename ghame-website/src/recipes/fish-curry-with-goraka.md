title: "Fish Curry with Goraka"
published: true
order: 20
summary: "A sour, gently spiced fish curry cooked in a clay pot."
image: /assets/img/illustrations/curry-pot.svg
image_alt: "Fish curry in a clay pot"
time: "40 minutes"
serves: "4"
spices:
  - fish-curry-powder
  - chilli-pieces
  - turmeric-powder
ingredients:
  - "500 g firm fish such as tuna or seer, cubed"
  - "3 pieces goraka, soaked in ½ cup hot water, then ground to a paste"
  - "2 tbsp Ghame Fish Curry Powder"
  - "1 tsp Ghame Chilli Pieces"
  - "½ tsp Ghame Turmeric Powder"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "1 tbsp coconut oil"
  - "1 red onion, sliced"
  - "3 cloves garlic and a small piece of ginger, chopped"
  - "2 green chillies and a sprig of curry leaves"
  - "1 cup thin coconut milk"
  - "½ cup thick coconut milk"
---
1. Mix the fish with the fish curry powder, chilli pieces, turmeric, salt and goraka paste. Leave for 15 minutes.
2. Heat the oil in a clay pot and fry the onion, garlic, ginger, green chillies and curry leaves until soft.
3. Add the fish with its marinade and the thin coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 10 minutes without stirring; shake the pot instead so the fish stays whole.
4. Add the thick coconut milk and simmer for 3 to 5 minutes. Adjust the salt.
