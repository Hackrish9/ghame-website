# Ghame website: staff guide

Portal: `https://ghame.lk/admin/`. Sign in with your GitHub account (ask the administrator to add you).

## How publishing works
1. Edit and press **Save**. The change is a draft.
2. Set the status to **In review**, then **Ready**. Cloudflare builds a preview link for every draft,
   so a second person can check it before it goes live (maker-checker).
3. Press **Publish**. The website updates in 1 to 2 minutes.

## Handling a WhatsApp order
Each website order arrives on WhatsApp with a number such as `GS-260918-4821`, the items, pack sizes, SKUs,
the customer's details and country.
1. Check stock for each line.
2. Work out the delivery charge: courier within Sri Lanka depends on the location; international depends on the country.
3. Reply with the final total and payment details.
4. After payment, dispatch and send the tracking details.
5. Reduce stock in the portal (see below). Stock is never reduced automatically, because many chats never become orders.

## Update stock (daily, and after every production run)
Shop: Products > open the product > **Pack sizes, prices and stock** > change **Stock** > Save > Publish.
The stock report at `/admin/stock/` lists low and out-of-stock pack sizes. Low stock is set in Settings > Store.

## Change a price
Same place as stock. Price 0 shows "Price on request". **Previous price** shows as "Was LKR ..." when higher.

## Add or remove a product
- Add: Shop: Products > **New Product**. Every pack size needs a unique SKU.
- Hide without deleting: untick **Published on the website**.
- Remove a pack size a product does not come in (for example 10 kg vanilla): delete that row.
- **Sold to**: *HORECA only* packs are marked HORECA in the shop and listed on the Ghame Professional page.

## Replace an image
Open the field and choose **Replace** or **Remove**. Use JPG or WebP, at least 1600 px wide, under 400 KB.
Always fill in the image description. Until a photo is added, the site shows a botanical illustration.

## Hide or show a homepage section
Website pages > Homepage > open the section > **Show**.

## Certifications
Settings > Business details > Certifications. A certificate shows only when **Verified** is ticked and the
expiry date has not passed. Next surveillance audit: **1 February 2027**. After each audit, upload the new
certificate PDF and preview image and update the dates.

## Verification register (monthly, PDCA)
Governance > Content verification register. Printable report: `/admin/register/`.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified current | Confirmed by management; may be published |
| Requires update | Published or listed, but out of date |
| Admin verification required | Needs a manager's check or evidence before it is relied on |
| Content required from Ghame | Nothing to publish yet |

Plan: assign owner and due date. Do: fix in the portal. Check: review the report monthly. Act: set Verified
current, record who approved it and the date.
