This website does not ask you to create an account and does not take payments.

**What we receive.** When you order or enquire, the website opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message written out. We receive only what you choose to send: usually your name, phone number, delivery address and the products you want.

**What stays on your device.** Your shopping cart is stored in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us until you send your order.

**How we use it.** We use your details to confirm, deliver and support your order, and to answer your enquiry. We do not sell your details.

**WhatsApp.** Messages sent through WhatsApp are also handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

**Your rights.** You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022. Contact us using the details on the Contact page.
