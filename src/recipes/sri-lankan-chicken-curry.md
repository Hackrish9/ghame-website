title: "Sri Lankan Chicken Curry"

order: 10
summary: "A Sunday chicken curry with roasted curry powder, coconut milk and curry leaves."
image: /assets/img/illustrations/curry-pot.svg
image_alt: "Chicken curry in a clay pot"
time: "50 minutes"
serves: "4"
spices:
  - roasted-curry-powder
  - chilli-powder
  - turmeric-powder
ingredients:
  - "1 kg chicken, cut into curry pieces"
  - "2 tbsp Ghame Roasted Curry Powder"
  - "1 tsp Ghame Chilli Powder"
  - "½ tsp Ghame Turmeric Powder"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "2 tbsp coconut oil"
  - "1 large red onion, sliced"
  - "4 cloves garlic and a thumb of ginger, chopped"
  - "2 green chillies, slit"
  - "1 sprig curry leaves and a piece of rampe"
  - "1 small cinnamon stick"
  - "2 tomatoes, chopped"
  - "1 cup thick coconut milk"
---
1. Rub the chicken with the curry powder, chilli powder, turmeric and salt. Leave for 20 minutes.
2. Heat the oil and fry the onion, garlic, ginger, green chillies, curry leaves, rampe and cinnamon until the onion is soft and golden.
3. Add the chicken, stir to coat it in the spices and cook on a medium flame for 5 minutes.
4. Add the tomatoes and half a cup of water. Cover and simmer for 25 minutes, stirring now and then.
5. Pour in the coconut milk and simmer uncovered for 5 to 10 minutes until the gravy thickens. Taste for salt.

Serve with rice, parippu and pol sambol.
