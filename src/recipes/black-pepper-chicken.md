---
title: "Black Pepper Chicken"
published: true
order: 30
summary: "A Sri Lankan pepper chicken where black pepper, not chilli, carries the heat."
image: /assets/img/illustrations/curry-pot.svg
image_alt: "Black pepper chicken in a clay pot"
time: "40 minutes"
serves: "4"
spices:
  - black-pepper-powder
  - turmeric-powder
ingredients:
  - "750 g boneless chicken, cubed"
  - "1½ tbsp Ghame Black Pepper Powder, plus more to taste"
  - "½ tsp Ghame Turmeric Powder"
  - "1 tsp salt"
  - "2 tbsp oil"
  - "1 large onion, sliced"
  - "5 cloves garlic, sliced, and a thumb of ginger"
  - "2 green chillies and a sprig of curry leaves"
  - "1 tbsp soy sauce (optional)"
  - "1 tsp lime juice"
---
1. Mix the chicken with half the pepper, the turmeric and the salt. Leave for 15 minutes.
2. Heat the oil and fry the onion, garlic, ginger, green chillies and curry leaves until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken and cook on a high flame for 8 to 10 minutes until browned and cooked through.
4. Add the rest of the pepper, the soy sauce and 2 to 3 tablespoons of water. Toss until the chicken is glossy and dry.
5. Finish with lime juice.
